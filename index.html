Screening Agent: fast yes, accountable no

Prototype for the Darwinbox take-home (D3). The agent answers every frontline applicant within minutes and books strong candidates straight into interviews. A “no” is only ever a published knockout rule the candidate can contest, or a recruiter’s decision. Every step writes a decision receipt. All data is synthetic. Scoring runs in your browser with deterministic rules; in production an LLM extracts the evidence (D2 R4).
